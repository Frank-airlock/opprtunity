🛡️ Stay safe — 30-second scam checklist (read before acting on any opportunity)
  • Never pay to get paid. Upfront “registration”, “activation”, or “withdrawal” fees = scam.
  • “Guaranteed profits” do not exist. Anyone guaranteeing returns is lying to you.
  • Never share your seed phrase or bank OTP — no legit airdrop or employer asks for these.
  • Be careful with WhatsApp/Telegram DMs. Verify on the official website, not in a chat.
  • Too good to be true usually is. “Double your money in 3 days” = run.
  • Do your own research (DYOR). Search the name + “scam” or “review” before committing time or money.
